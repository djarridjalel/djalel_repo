### Logo points the book must resolve

- One-color version: the grey (#666) fold needs a solid variant for stencils, embroidery and one-color print.
- Minimum size: the arrow detail closes up below about 30 px tall; "Logistics" disappears at small sizes. Define a monogram-only version.
- File hygiene: the X polygon is drawn twice in the SVG.

## Touchpoints at launch

- Warehouses and signage: façades, racking labels, safety and wayfinding signs, docks
- Fleet and handling gear: trucks, forklifts, containers, pallets
- Uniforms and PPE: workwear, high-visibility vests, helmets, badges
- Documents and digital: quotes, invoices, delivery notes, website, client portal, LinkedIn
