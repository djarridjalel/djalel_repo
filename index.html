Case 01PharmaceuticalEvolab Laboratories

A full pharmaceutical catalogue that had to read as scientifically credible and future-facing at the same time — mature enough to trust, current enough to feel new.

A compressed circle unfurls into a perfect one.

Role
Creative Director
Sector
Pharmaceutical
Market
Algeria
Scope
Identity · Packaging · Exhibition · Digital
Client
Evolab Laboratories
Yellow-green → deep blue
Mark

Three modes — gradient, tricolour, single-colour — because the same mark had to survive a screen, a printed carton and one ink on blister foil without becoming a different brand in each.

Typography

Futura against a custom display face. Geometry reads as science; the drawn face keeps it from reading as a hospital.

Colour

The palette runs yellow-green to deep blue — the unfurling again, in tone rather than in line.

Packaging

Five SKUs — Evofenid, Evopiramide, Lansoprotect, Xyline, Omeprotect. Each regulation-compliant on its own, and unmistakably one family on a shelf.

Environment

A full exhibition stand for Algeria’s leading pharmaceutical trade show, carried from 3D renders through to the on-site build.

Digital

evolabdz.com — bilingual, UX/UI direction, delivered with a development partner.

Case 02ParapharmaceuticalNatural Solution

A natural-origin story that still had to read as clinically credible — botanical enough to be believed, disciplined enough not to tip into decoration.

Three leaves, arranged to form the letter T.

Role
Creative Director
Sector
Parapharmaceutical
Duration
3-year partnership
Scope
Identity · Packaging direction · Social
Client
Natural Solution
Deep ocean blue / yellow-green
Mark

Six formats — horizontal, vertical, compact, social, print, simplified. A mark that appears on a jar lid, a pharmacy shelf and a phone screen is really three marks; drawing all six up front stopped the brand improvising them later.

Typography

Gilroy — humanist enough to carry the botanical claim, geometric enough to hold the clinical one.

Colour

Deep ocean blue against yellow-green, and the green is never allowed to stand alone. That single rule is what keeps the range out of health-food territory.

Packaging

Direction across a catalogue that grew past thirty products without the system being redrawn once.

Social

Promotional and presentation templates, so three years of output stayed recognisably one brand.

A flexible identity built to scale alongside the brand’s expanding catalogue — and it has, for three years.

Case 03Dermo-cosmeticLaformul + BioFormul

Built before the brand existed. No product, no name on a shelf, no audience — strategy and identity defined from day one, then a second brand born out of the first one working.

Precision in black and white. Then two linked circles.

Role
Brand builder, from zero
Sector
Dermo-cosmetic
Client
Les Laboratoires Pro-Icon
Scope
Two full identity systems
Span
Two years apart
60 / 30 / 10 — grey, grey, gold
Laformul

Bold black type, generous white space, iridescent product textures across a serum, SPF and cream range. The restraint was the argument: a new name earns trust by looking certain.

BioFormul

Two linked circles — science and care in continuous motion. Launched two years later, once Laformul had proved there was room for a science-led extension.

Typography

Gilroy across both, so the pair read as siblings rather than as a brand and its imitator.

Colour

A disciplined 60/30/10 — grey, grey, gold. The gold is the tenth, which is what stops the science from turning cold.

Architecture

SKU-level colour logic — gold, orange, multi-tone — so each formula tells its own story without leaving the system.
