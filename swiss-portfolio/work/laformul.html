Dermo-cosmetic

Laformul + BioFormul

No product, no name and no audience at the start. I built the brand from day one, and two years later its sales justified a second, science-led brand.

My roleBrand lead, strategy to print
ClientLes Laboratoires Pro-Icon
Team[n] designers
SpanTwo launches, two years apart
ScopeTwo identity systems, packaging
TypefaceGilroy across both
The brief

Les Laboratoires Pro-Icon wanted to enter dermo-cosmetics with nothing on the shelf. A new name has no track record, so the design had to carry the credibility on its own.

Two years later the client came back for BioFormul, a more science-led range that had to look related to Laformul without looking like a copy.

Constraints
No track recordAnything decorative reads as compensation, so the brand relies on restraint.
Sibling, not copyBioFormul must read as the same house without diluting Laformul.
Colour discipline60/30/10: grey, grey and gold across both brands.
Formula codingEach SKU needs its own colour inside the system.
Process
  1. StrategyPositioning and naming before any design.
  2. Laformul identityBold black type, white space, iridescent textures.
  3. Packaging architectureSKU colour logic: gold, orange, multi-tone.
  4. BioFormulTwo linked circles on the same typeface and 60/30/10 rule.
  5. LaunchSerum, SPF and cream first, the supplement range two years later.

Relevant for your team
